About the Scheme

The Old Age Pension Scheme is implemented by the Department of Social Welfare, Government of Purvanchal, under the State Social Assistance Programme.

Eligibility

Residents of Purvanchal who are 60 years of age or older on the date of application may apply, subject to the applicable scheme guidelines.

Application Limit

Only one application is allowed per eligible citizen.

Application Processing

Applications must be processed within 15 days under the applicable Purvanchal Right to Public Services Act. Applications that remain unprocessed beyond the statutory deadline are subject to deemed approval and should be displayed accordingly.
